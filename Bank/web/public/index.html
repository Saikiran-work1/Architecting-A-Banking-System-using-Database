Settlement Controls & Rules

  • Atomic Execution Sender debit, recipient credit, and ledger entry commit together or roll back completely.
  • Active Account Verification Both originating and destination accounts must carry an ACTIVE status.

  • Fixed-Point Currency Guard Amounts with more than two decimal places or non-positive values are rejected.
